The terroir

Cooked by the mountain

Our chefs are detail-oriented in the quiet sense: they know where the herbs were grown, how long the dal has been simmering, and exactly when the bread left the oven.

“Himachali Dham, a Thai curry and a glass of something cold — the mountain does not ask you to choose.”

Alongside the indulgences, there is a thoughtful menu for fitness enthusiasts: meals that are both tempting and healthy, designed for people who want to return home feeling lighter than when they arrived. Savour the rich flavours of Himachali Dham, prepared with locally sourced ingredients, amidst the mountains.
