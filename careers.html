Join our circle

Work where others escape

Hospitality at Moksha is different from a bustling city hotel. Here, luxury is quiet, unhurried, and deeply rooted in the natural silence of the Himalayas.

“We do not just host guests; we restore them. That begins with a team that feels valued, supported, and at home.”

As part of Asia Resorts Limited, our team members enjoy competitive remuneration, continuous professional training, comfortable staff accommodations at the property, and the unique privilege of living and working surrounded by pine forests at 5,000 ft.
