Holi Celebration Package near Delhi

By Moksha Editorial · 12 Feb 2026 · 559 words

As the vibrant hues of Holi paint India in a joyous spectacle, are you seeking a getaway that offers both the festivity of the occasion and a chance to rejuvenate? Look no further than Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort, a haven of luxury nestled amidst the breathtaking Shivalik Ranges in Parwanoo, Himachal Pradesh with best holi celebration package near Delhi.

Located just a short distance from Delhi (approximately a 5-6 hour drive), Moksha provides the perfect escape for those seeking a tranquil retreat near the Delhi. While Delhi itself can be quite lively during Holi, with vibrant street celebrations and colorful processions, Moksha offers a serene counterpoint, allowing you to celebrate the spirit of Holi in a more peaceful setting.

A Luxurious Escape Awaits near Delhi

Perched at an elevation of 5,000 feet, Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort  is the the luxurious celebration getaway near Delhi, boasts stunning panoramic views of the lush green mountains. Imagine waking up to the symphony of birdsong and breathing in the fresh mountain air – the ideal way to start your Holi celebration.

Moksha is renowned for its exceptional offerings, making it a top luxury resort in Himachal Pradesh. Here’s what awaits you:

  • Ayurvedic Spa: Indulge in the ancient practice of Ayurveda, a holistic healing system, at Moksha’s renowned spa. Rejuvenate your mind and body with a selection of personalized treatments designed to restore balance and promote well-being.
  • Infinity Pool: Take a dip in the resort’s mesmerizing infinity pool, seemingly merging with the majestic mountainscape. Relax and soak in the breathtaking views while surrounded by the serenity of the Himalayas.
  • Cable Car: Experience the thrill of a cable car ride, offering a panoramic perspective of the surrounding valleys and hills. This unique experience adds a touch of adventure to your Holi getaway.
  • Culinary Delights: Embark on a culinary journey at Moksha’s acclaimed restaurants. Savor delectable dishes ranging from traditional Himachali cuisine to international fare, all prepared with fresh, locally-sourced ingredients.

Celebrate the Spirit of Holi near Delhi

While Moksha provides a tranquil escape near Delhi, you can still embrace the spirit of Holi with traditional elements. Light an eco-friendly bonfire, create colorful Rangoli patterns (decorative floor art), and enjoy delicious Holi delicacies prepared by the resort’s chefs.

Unwind and Rejuvenate

After the festivities, unwind and pamper yourself with the resort’s exceptional amenities. Relax by the pool, indulge in a rejuvenating spa treatment (at a discounted price!), or simply soak in the breathtaking views from your balcony. Moksha’s high-speed internet access ensures you can stay connected if needed, while our convenient laundry service (with a special 20% discount during your Holi stay) takes care of those travel essentials.
