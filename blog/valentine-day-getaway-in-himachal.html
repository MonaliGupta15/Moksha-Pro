Romantic Retreats: Exploring the Enchanting Hill Stations of Himachal for Valentine's Day

Seeking a romantic escape in Himachal for Valentine’s Day? Parwanoo, a hill station nestled in the Shivalik ranges, offers a captivating blend of natural beauty, adventure activities, and romantic experiences, making it the perfect destination for a Valentine’s Day escape. 

Here are some things you can do to make your Valentine’s Day in Parwanoo, Himachal truly special:

Delight in a romantic journey on the cable car. Soar above the lush valleys and witness breathtaking panoramic views of the Himalayas on a romantic cable car ride at Timber Trail Resort. This 3.6-kilometer ride offers stunning vistas, making it a perfect way to set the mood for your Valentine’s Day celebration.

Spend a Night Under the Stars: Moksha Resort in Parwanoo offers the opportunity to spend a night under the starlit sky. Imagine setting up a tent, cuddling up with your loved one, and gazing at the Milky Way – a truly unforgettable Valentine’s Day experience.

Rejuvenate your body and soul: Immerse yourselves in the tranquil sanctuary of our Ayurvedic spa, choosing from a selection of rejuvenating treatments designed to reconnect you with each other and nature’s healing touch. Avail a special 10% discount on all spa services during your Valentine’s week stay.

Take a Boat Ride on Gobind Sagar Lake: Gobind Sagar Lake, located near Bhakra Nangal, offers serene boat rides amidst stunning Himalayan scenery. Enjoy a peaceful ride with your loved one, taking in the beauty of the lake and the surrounding mountains. 

Take a Hot Air Balloon Ride: For a truly unique and unforgettable experience, consider taking a hot air balloon ride over the Himalayas. Soar above the valleys and witness breathtaking panoramic views of the snow-capped mountains – a truly magical way to celebrate your love.

But wait, there’s more! Elevate your Valentine’s escape with these experiences:

  • Sunrise yoga session: Greet the day together with a revitalizing yoga session overlooking the Himalayas, followed by a blissful breakfast in your room.
  • Private jacuzzi experience: Unwind in the soothing bubbles of your own private jacuzzi, enjoying stunning views and each other’s company.
  • Nature walk with a picnic: Embark on a romantic walk through the lush Himalayan foothills, culminating in a delightful picnic amidst breathtaking scenery.

This Valentine’s Day, escape to Moksha Resort & Spa and let the magic of the Himachal rekindle the flames of your love. Book your Valentine’s Week Getaway today and create memories that will last a lifetime.

Himachal offers something for everyone, making it the perfect destination for a romantic and memorable Valentine’s Day celebration. Choose the activities that appeal to you and your loved one, and create a Valentine’s Day that you’ll cherish forever.
