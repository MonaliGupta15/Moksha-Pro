Top Summer Holiday Destinations in India

By Moksha Editorial · 21 May 2025 · 924 words

With summer temperatures rising across the Plains of India, scores of travellers scour town and country for the best summer holiday destinations to beat the sun. Yes, hill stations…

With summer temperatures rising across the Plains of India, scores of travellers scour town and country for the best summer holiday destinations to beat the sun. Yes, hill stations of Himachal, including Shimla, Manali and Mussoorie, attract everyone but sitting in solitude in Parwanoo could be more soothing, serene, scenic and rejuvenating.

Parwanoo is tucked in the lower Shivalik ranges and is an underrated treasure if you want to escape to some place away from the regular tourist hubs. Offering everything from lush green hills, cable car rides, orchards, and wellness retreats, this mountain has everything you need for an amazing summer vacation. You plan a romantic escape, a solo retreat or a family holiday; Parwanoo is one of the best summer holiday destinations.

Why Choose Parwanoo for Your Summer Vacation?

1. Close Proximity to Major Cities

Great connectivity makes Parwanoo one of the best summer travel destinations. This lovely hill station is placed just about 35 km from Chandigarh and close to 270 km from Delhi. This makes it an ideal summer weekend escape from Delhi or a quick escape from Chandigarh.

2. Pleasant Weather & Scenic Beauty

During the summer months, Parwanoo experiences a moderate and comfortable weather pattern, due to which one can easily rescue oneself from the sun’s scorching heat that affects major cities. Cool breeze combined with panoramic views of the hills makes for a tranquil place that is not only difficult to find but also hard to forget.  

3. Adventure & Activities for All Ages

There are trekking trails and nature walks to explore, orchards to explore and Asia’s longest cable car, where you can ride. Don’t miss the thrilling ride on the Timber Trail Cable Car, which offers breathtaking views of the valley and surrounding hills.

4. Apple Orchards & Local Delights

Also famous for fruit orchards, Parwanoo is especially known for apples and peaches. In the summer months, the orchards are alive, as people can visit for fresh produce, jams and juices which are available direct from the source. These orchards offer great picnic spots and also give you a chance to engage with the local culture and farming traditions.

Best Things to Do in Parwanoo in Summer

1. Timber Trail Cable Car

Riding the Timber Trail Cable Car is a highlight of any Parwanoo visit, offering jaw-dropping views across the mountain valley. It’s not only transportation; it’s an experience. There is nothing like hovering above pine-covered slopes as the cool breeze feels on your hair. For many, it is the best way to get their summer holiday in Parwanoo.

2. Nature Walks and Forest Trails

Parwanoo’s forest trails are just fine for lazy walks or short hikes during the summer months. There is enough rich biodiversity and peaceful surroundings that birdwatchers and photography enthusiasts will be thrilled. Morning or evening walks early in the day are a chance to see the area’s flora and fauna under a green canopy.

3. Visit to Kali Mata Temple

The Kali Mata Temple sitting on a hilltop provides not only the spiritual respite but also a magnificent panoramic view of Parwanoo. Sunrise or sunset, it’s a great spot and is easy to access by the short trek or the road.

4. Enjoy Local Cuisine

Parwanoo serves a taste of Himachali as well as North Indian cuisine. Don’t miss out on Madra, Siddu and local Rajma chawal during your summer break in Parwanoo. With organic, locally sourced ingredients, many resorts in Parwanoo offer these authentic Hmachali delicacies.

Best Time to Visit Parwanoo

Summer (April to June) is a particularly good time to visit Parwanoo, though, as it is a year-round destination. The temperature of these months is pleasant, about 15°C to 30°C. The air is sunny but not harsh, and the evenings are inviting and cool.

Get the best out of summer holidays in Parwanoo if you plan your trip between April and early July.  

Where to Stay: Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort – The Ultimate Summer Retreat

Looking for the best place to stay in Parwanoo on your summer vacation? Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort is the ultimate go-to option. Situated high above the valley, this resort can be reached via its own private cable car and provides a one-of-a-kind experience perfect for those looking to unite comfort, wellness and all the beautiful views it has to offer.

  • Holistic Spa Treatments: Moksha offers holistic spa treatments – Ayurvedic massages to international therapies – a sanctuary for the mind and body.
  • Infinity Pool with a View: Enjoy an infinity pool with a view – the perfect summer indulgence. Take a dip with lush green hills in your sight.
  • Fine Dining: From fresh, local ingredients, prepared into multi-cuisine delicious meals.
  • Experiential Packages: Yoga sessions, meditation classes, nature walks and bonfire nights make for an unforgettable experience out of your holiday.

Final Thoughts

India offers many summer holiday destinations, but none as easily accessible, beautiful and peaceful as Parwanoo. This Himachali town is a land of scenic views and orchard visits to forest trails and wellness retreats that promise a soul-soothed escape. If you’re planning your next summer vacation in India, you may like to throw in a visit to this quiet little retreat in the Shivalik hills.

And when you do, make sure to book your stay at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort for an unforgettable experience that blends nature, luxury, and rejuvenation – the perfect formula for a dream summer holiday.
