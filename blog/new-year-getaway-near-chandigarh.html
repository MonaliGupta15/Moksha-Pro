New Year Getaway Near Chandigarh: A Perfect Escape to Start the Year Right

By Moksha Editorial · 12 Dec 2025 · 813 words

With New Year’s Eve around the corner, people need a break from urban cities and to organise memorable celebrations. The good news for travellers seeking a New Year getaway near Chandigarh will be delighted, as there are so many options to choose from. The nearby areas of Chandigarh have wonderful sites, including a hill station, luxurious resorts for accommodation, and beautiful places where one would love to begin the new year. No matter whether you are looking for a quiet vacation in nature or colourful celebrations, a new year getaway near Chandigarh is a perfect escape to start the coming year.

Best Places for a New Year Getaway Near Chandigarh

Parwanoo- Parwanoo is another place that is closely located to Chandigarh and will prove to be a great place to celebrate the new year. To escape the fast-developing concrete jungle, Parwanoo turns out ot be an oasis with a natural green environment and beautiful  scenery. If you want to experience everything to perfection, go for Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort, a  retreat of luxury and relaxation.

Shimla- Famously called the “Queen of Hills,” Shimla is traditional but fits the New Year break perfectly. This is the right place for celebration due to the snow-capped mountains, the colonial structure of the buildings, and the New Year’s Eve parties on Mall Road. Some of the fun aspects include ice skating, trekking, and sometimes a barbeque along with a bonfire under the stars.

Kasauli- Though this small hill station is only an hour’s drive from Chandigarh, it offers the perfect quaint ambiance one needs to get away from the busy schedule of metro cities. The combination of the beautiful and calm surroundings, serene paths and tracks, and charming, cute markets makes the place appropriate for people to take time and reflect on the past year. Don’t forget ‘Sunset at Monkey Point’ to wrap up the day!

Why Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort is the Perfect Choice

Situated within the beauty of Shivalik Hills, Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort is a perfect place to stay where the luxury of peace and calm envelopes the soul. The place is situated at a spot that can be reached only through an aerial cable car, making the whole journey an experience in itself. After reaching  there, you will be greeted by stunning visuals, a high standard of service, and an experience that radiates serenity.

Luxurious Accommodations- The resort comprises ample furnished rooms for guests with a view to achieve comfort and style. Each of the private balconies opens out to a breathtaking view of the hills surrounding the hills and makes each room a private space for relaxation. The cosy interiors, modern amenities, and thoughtful details add charm.

Wellness at Its Best- True to its name, Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort offers an exceptional range of wellness treatments. From traditional Ayurvedic massages to modern therapies, their spa is the perfect retreat to detox your body and mind before stepping into the New Year.

New Year Celebrations Like No Other- Moksha takes New Year celebrations to the next level with specially curated experiences. Picture yourself enjoying a bonfire under the stars, live music filling the crisp mountain air, and a lavish gala dinner featuring a wide range of delectable dishes. For those seeking quiet moments, the infinity pool and serene walking trails offer a peaceful escape.

Unmatched Dining Experiences- The resort’s dining options are equally impressive. Interesting flavours ranging from local delights to international cuisines make every meal fun. Do not miss the exclusive New Year menu, which beautifully enhances festive flavours with culinary artistry.

Activities for All –Venture alone, with your partner, or perhaps if you are travelling with family, Moksha has activities suitable for one and all. Nature walks, yoga, and fun games ensure your stay is never short of adventure.

This New Year, gift yourself a memorable experience. With scenic views, lavish amenities, and tailored celebrations, Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort is, without any doubt, the best escape close to Chandigarh. Why look back? Book yourself now for a bright and refreshing start to the new year! 

FAQs About New Year Getaways Near Chandigarh

1. What is the best time to book a New Year getaway near Chandigarh?

The suggested time frame for such trips is probably to start about two months prior to the day.  Moreover, prime spots like Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort get booked almost instantly.

2.What are some family-friendly New Year destinations near Chandigarh?

Shimla, Kasauli, and Parwanoo are well-visited family-friendly options. Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort is ideal for families with amenities and activities for kids.

3. What sort of activities can I do while on my New Year break from Chandigarh?

Depending on the destination, you can enjoy activities like trekking, sightseeing, spa treatments, bonfires, and live music. At Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort, you can indulge in wellness therapies, infinity pool relaxation, festive dinners, and exclusive New Year events.
