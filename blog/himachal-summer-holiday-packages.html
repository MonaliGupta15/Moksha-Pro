Himachal Summer Holiday Packages

If you are looking for a refreshing summer holiday package that helps you escape the heat, then Parwanoo is the ideal destination. Nestled in the Shivalik range of Himachal Pradesh, this charming hill station is known for its beauty and blend of relaxation, adventure, and soul-soothing nature. Whether you seek peace, love nature, or are an adventure enthusiast, Parwanoo will have something for everyone.

Why Choose Parwanoo for Your Summer Holiday?

Parwanoo is a tranquil hill station often admired for its lush greenery, pleasant weather, and splendid views. This captivating destination is relatively less popular; hence, it provides an escape from the hustle and bustle of the city and is perfect for travellers looking for peace. The luxury resorts, scenic trails, and adventure activities make it even more alluring.

Best Attractions in Parwanoo

  1. Timber Trail – Enjoy a thrilling cable car ride while savouring the views of the valley.
  2. Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort – EUnwind with spa treatments in a truly luxurious and tranquil ambiance.
  3. Kalka-Shimla Toy Train – Go on a heritage train ride and enjoy the picturesque landscapes.
  4. Fruit Orchards – Visit beautiful farms and orchards of peaches and apples to experience fresh fruits right from the farm.
  5. Dagshai – Stroll around the beautiful cantonment town and soak in the British architecture around the town.

Make the most out of your summer vacation this year by opting for customised packages that incorporate sightseeing, relaxation, and adventure for a wholesome experience.

1. Adventure & Nature Retreat Package

  • Stay at a luxurious resort in Parwanoo
  • Timber Trail cable car ride with stunning views.
  • Guided nature walks & trekking in the lush green trails
  • Bonfire & stargazing at night

  • Guided tours to local villages for a cultural experience

Best Time to Visit Parwanoo

The period from March to June marks the coolest season in Parwanoo and is ideal for a summer holiday. During this time, the temperatures range from 15°C to 30°C, making it a perfect getaway from the summer heat.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the best time to visit Parwanoo for a summer holiday?

Catch Parwanoo in its most beautiful state by visiting during the months of March to June.  

2. What are the top attractions in Parwanoo?

The must-see tourism spots for Parwanoo are the Timber Trail, Kalka-Shimla Toy Train, fruit orchards, and Dagshai Cantonment.

3. What are the adventure activities available in Parwanoo?

Other activities to do in Parwanoo include cable car rides, trekking, nature walks, fireside evenings, and much more!

4. Which is the best resort for a summer holiday in Parwanoo?

If you want to fully spoil yourself during your stay in Parwanoo, Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort is the best choice without a doubt. 

5. How can I reach Parwanoo?

Getting to Parwanoo is relatively easy as it is connected by road and rail. There is a railway station in Kalka (3 km) and Chandigarh Airport is 35 km.

6. What kind of summer holiday packages are available in Parwanoo?

You can choose from adventure, wellness, and cultural packages, depending on your preference.

7. Are there spa and wellness retreats in Parwanoo?

Yes, Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort offers world-class spa treatments, yoga, and wellness programs.

8. What makes Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort the best stay option?

This luxury spa resort offers breathtaking views, wellness treatments, an infinity pool, and fine dining, making it the perfect summer retreat.
