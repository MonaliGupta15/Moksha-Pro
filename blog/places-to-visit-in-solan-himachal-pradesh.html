Discover the Best Places to Visit in Solan, Himachal Pradesh

Solan is nestled in the fertile hills of the Himachal Pradesh, providing a peaceful and lively blend of nature, colonial legacy, sacred places and thrill. If you are planning to visit Solan (either in the short term or the long term), below are the best spots to visit in Solan, the best time to visit and the best way to maximise your stay.

What Makes Solan Special

  • Solan is a place situated between Chandigarh and Shimla. Hence, it has good connectivity.
  • It also possesses a pleasant climate through the whole year with a cool summer, crisp winter and lush monsoon vegetation.
  • Full of culture, temples, forts, monasteries and sanctuaries.

Top Places to Visit in Solan, Himachal Pradesh

Exploring Solan in Himachal Pradesh, one can see a number of attractions that are the combination of history, spirituality, nature and colonial charm. Below is a description of the places that are a must-see in and around the town:

  1. Mohan Shakti National Heritage Park

 This heritage complex is situated in the area about 15 km north of Solan. It is considered one of the most remarkable attractions in Solan. It offers well-designed temples, full-size figures of Vedic rishis, elaborate Hindu designs, and majestic gardens. Its serene surroundings ensure that it is a good recreational area to have a stroll and reflect in peace.

  1. Shoolini Mata Temple

The Shoolini Mata Temple is a sacred temple of the goddess Shoolini and is located right in the middle of Solan town. It is a spiritual centre also as well as a centre of the yearly Shoolini Fair. The fair is attended by thousands of faithfuls and travellers annually. The temple is a great place to visit due to its sacred experience as well as the panoramic visuals of the town.

  1. Majathal Sanctuary

Majathal Sanctuary is a nature and wildlife sanctuary about 26-30 km to the southeast of Solan. The sanctuary, located on rough grounds, carries the endangered Cheer Pheasant and some other bird species as well as wild animals. Its forest paths ensure it is the best place to go on a trekking and photography trip.

  1. Kuthar Fort

It is an 800 years old historical marvel and is located about 34 km away from Solan. The complex of the fort has ancient temples, natural springs, and beautiful gardens that present a rare combination of tradition and nature. The antique nature and serene environment make it a satisfying field visit when you are a history lover.

  1. Menri Monastery

Menri Monastery is one of the oldest Bon monasteries in the world. It is one of the incredible historical and religious sites in the world near Solan. With its colourful buildings and calm atmosphere, it is ideal in case one wants to relax or visit the Tibetan-Bon culture. The monastery is readily available in town and it provides spiritual comfort and breathtaking scenes of the adjacent hills.

Best Time to Visit Solan

Season What to Expect Pros & Cons
Spring (March-May) Pleasant weather, budding green landscape, ideal for treks & outdoor sightseeing. Pro: Clear skies, mild temps. Con: Can get crowded in peak times.
Summer (June-July) Warm but cooler than the plains, lush greenery, waterfalls flow strong. Pro: Escapes the heat of cities. Con: Occasional heavy rains/landslides during monsoon onset.
Autumn (September-November) Crisp air, changing foliage, fewer tourists. Pro: Very scenic, good for photographers. Con: Nights can get cold.
Winter (December-February) Cold, possibly snow in surrounding areas, festive mood, fewer crowds. Pro: Quiet, peaceful, scenic. Con: Some roads or higher routes may be less accessible.
