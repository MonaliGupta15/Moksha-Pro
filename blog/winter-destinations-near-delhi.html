Exploring Offbeat Winter Destinations Near Delhi

By Moksha Editorial · 22 Oct 2024 · 871 words

Winter is the perfect time to escape the bustling city of Delhi and retreat to a serene destination where you can enjoy the cold weather, snow-covered landscapes, and peaceful surroundings. While popular spots like Shimla and Manali tend to get overcrowded, there are several offbeat winter destinations near Delhi that offer an equally magical experience with fewer tourists. These hidden gems provide a unique blend of natural beauty, adventure, and tranquillity, making them ideal for a memorable winter getaway.

Parwanoo, Himachal Pradesh

As the winter chill settles in, Parwanoo, a hidden gem in Himachal Pradesh, is the perfect destination for a peaceful escape from Delhi. Just 270 km from the city, Parwanoo offers a tranquil retreat where you can soak in the beauty of snow-dusted landscapes, explore apple orchards, and enjoy an unhurried pace that makes for an unforgettable winter experience. Unlike the more crowded hill stations, Parwanoo promises a serene ambiance, making it ideal for travellers seeking relaxation and a closer connection with nature.

Cable Car Ride

One of the most exciting experiences in Parwanoo is the famous Timber Trail cable car ride, which transports you over the beautiful Shivalik Hills, revealing breathtaking views of snow-clad valleys and forests. The aerial view of the snow-covered landscape is simply magical, creating a picturesque scene straight out of a postcard. This unique vantage point allows you to appreciate the beauty of Parwanoo from above, offering an experience that’s both thrilling and peaceful.

Winter Activities

For those looking to explore further, Parwanoo is home to a range of winter activities that allow you to truly immerse yourself in the area’s natural beauty. You can wander through apple orchards, hike along serene trails, or simply enjoy the crisp mountain air while savouring panoramic views of the surrounding hills. Parwanoo is also an excellent destination for nature photography, with its blend of rugged landscapes and peaceful winter scenery providing the perfect backdrop for capturing memorable moments.

Other Offbeat Destinations near Delhi

If you’re up for a broader winter adventure, Uttarakhand offers charming towns like Kanatal and Lansdowne, where snow-capped peaks and dense forests set the scene for a peaceful escape. Chakrata, too, is a winter gem with its lush forests and serene spots, and Nahan presents Himachal’s old-world charm through colonial architecture and scenic landscapes. But for a unique winter experience close to Delhi, Parwanoo offers the perfect combination of natural beauty, adventure, and relaxation. Pack your bags and embark on a rejuvenating winter escape to Parwanoo for a memorable season.

FAQs

1.What are some offbeat winter destinations near Delhi?

Some of the best offbeat winter destinations near Delhi include Kanatal, Chakrata, Lansdowne, Nahan, Shoghi, and Parwanoo. These places offer peaceful retreats with stunning landscapes and fewer crowds compared to popular tourist spots.

2. How far are these destinations from Delhi?

The distances vary, but most of these offbeat destinations are within a 250-350 km range from Delhi, making them perfect for a weekend getaway:

  • Kanatal: Around 300 km
  • Chakrata: Around 320 km
  • Lansdowne: Around 250 km
  • Nahan: Around 270 km
  • Shoghi: Around 330 km
  • Parwanoo: Around 270 km

3. What accommodation options are available at these destinations?

Each destination offers a range of accommodations, from budget stays to luxury resorts. If you’re visiting Parwanoo, Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort is a luxurious option with breathtaking views, an infinity pool, and spa services.

4. How can I reach these offbeat destinations?

Most of these destinations can be reached by road from Delhi. You can either drive yourself or hire a cab. Some places, like Chakrata and Lansdowne, are also accessible by train or bus, with nearby railway stations or bus services available.

5. Are these destinations crowded during the winter?

Unlike popular tourist spots, these offbeat destinations are relatively less crowded, making them perfect for travellers looking for peace and quiet during the winter season.

6. What are the must-see attractions in these places?

Each destination has its own unique attractions:

  • Parwanoo: Timber Trail and cable car rides.
  • Chakrata: Tiger Falls, Deoban, and local treks.
  • Lansdowne: Tip-n-Top viewpoint, Bhulla Lake, and St. Mary’s Church.
  • Nahan: Renuka Lake, Suketi Fossil Park, and Jaitak Fort.
  • Shoghi: Tara Devi Temple and surrounding pine forests.
  • Kanatal: Surkanda Devi Temple, Kodia Jungle, and Adventure Camps.
