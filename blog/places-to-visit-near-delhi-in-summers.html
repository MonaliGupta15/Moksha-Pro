Beat the Heat: Must-Visit Summer Getaways Near Delhi

By Moksha Editorial · 15 Apr 2024 · 356 words

When the Delhi summer descends with its scorching sun and relentless heat, the yearning for cooler climes becomes irresistible. Thankfully, the city is blessed with a plethora of places to visit near delhi in summers. These paradises, ranging from hill stations to wildlife sanctuaries, offer a refreshing escape within a comfortable driving distance.

For those seeking a quick summer weekend getaway from delhi, there are several charming options. Kasauli, nestled amidst the verdant hills of Himachal Pradesh, is a haven of tranquility. This cantonment town boasts colonial architecture, breathtaking views of the valley below, and a salubrious climate – perfect for beating the Delhi heat. Explore its charming lanes, unwind amidst the serenity of Monkey Point, or embark on a nature trail through the lush greenery.

Another gem for a Delhi weekend getaway in summer is Parwanoo. This Himachal Pradesh town, cradled in the embrace of the Shivalik Hills, offers stunning vistas and a welcome respite from the scorching plains. Parwanoo is an ideal base for exploring the nearby hills or indulging in adventure activities like paragliding.

For a truly luxurious escape, consider Moksha Resort & Spa. Nestled amidst the majestic Shivalik Ranges in Parwanoo, this resort offers an unparalleled experience. Perched at an elevation of 5000 feet, Moksha boasts breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains and a verdant tapestry of nature. This haven of tranquility is perfect for those seeking both relaxation and adventure. This resort boasts an elevation of 5000 feet, ensuring cool environs. Here, amidst the lush greenery, unwind at the renowned Ayurvedic spa, take a ride on the exhilarating cable car, or simply soak in the stunning views from the infinity pool. Moksha’s culinary experiences promise to tantalize your taste buds, while the serene atmosphere allows you to unwind and rejuvenate your mind, body, and soul.

Summer getaways from Delhi cater to all budgets and preferences. Whether you seek a luxurious retreat like Moksha Resort & Spa, a charming hill station like Kasauli, there’s a perfect esc ape waiting for you just a short journey away. So, pack your bags, escape the heat, and embark on a refreshing summer adventure near Delhi!
