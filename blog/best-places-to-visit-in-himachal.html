Best Places to Visit in Himachal in Summer

By Moksha Editorial · 10 Mar 2025 · 989 words

Catch between the sky and beautiful landscapes, the Himalayas embrace the awe-inspiring nature of Parwanoo. This town is a tranquil retreat nestled in the block of Himachal Pradesh, making it ideal for summer escapes alongside adventure outings. With its scenic vistas, adventure services, and avenues for relaxation, Parwanoo attracts a multitude of tourists. From colourful greenery and picturesque cable car rides to serene spa retreats, Parwanoo does not disappoint.

Why Visit Parwanoo in Summer?

  • Pleasant Weather: The summer in Parwanoo comes with moderately controlled temperatures coupled with cool refreshing breezes that add range to your escape.
  • Scenic Beauty: Sky-high views will encompass you even after the sun has set, with stunning beauty of the sprawling hills coming into sight.
  • Adventure & Fun: Parwanoo is famous for its cable car rides, trekking trails, and nature walks, perfect for adventure seekers.
  • Luxury Retreats: It is home to Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort, offering a blend of relaxation, wellness, and breathtaking views.

Top Things to Do in Parwanoo in Summer

1. Take a Ride on the Timber Trail Cable Car

Timber Trail is among the most bustling spots within Parwanoo. It offers stunning views of the Shivalik Hills and deep valleys, which is always a plus. The 1.8-kilometre cable car ride will take you to enthralling paragliding hilltop resorts. Imagine enjoying the beauty of the rolling hills—truly mesmerising.

Why You Should Try It:
One of the longest and most scenic cable car rides in India.
Offers stunning sunset and sunrise views.
Perfect for photography and adventure enthusiasts.

2. Explore the Kali Mata Temple

Situated on top of a hill, the Kali Mata Temple is one of the most popular pilgrimage places in Parwanoo. Strikingly architectural and magnificent, the temple is dedicated to Goddess Kali, whose divine presence calms its visitors. Guests can also admire the breathtaking view of the nearby mountains.

Why You Should Visit:
A perfect blend of spirituality and scenic beauty.
Ideal for a short trek or a peaceful walk.
Less crowded compared to other temple sites.

3. Indulge in a Nature Walk in the Pine Forests

Nature enthusiasts will find Parwanoo’s dense pine and deodar forests captivating. A stroll through the undisturbed woods is therapeutic, invigorating and tranquil—one can inhale the crisp mountain air and sight exotic birds. 

Best Time for Nature Walks:
Early morning for cool weather and chirping birds.
Late evening for a refreshing mountain breeze.

4. Visit the Gorkha Fort

Gorkha Fort is a short drive from Parwanoo, making it a perfect addition to your trip. Fans of history would appreciate the old fort and its beautiful view of the Himalayan foothills, as it helps understand the military history of the region.

Highlights of Gorkha Fort:
This interesting fort was built by the Gorkha rulers and stands as an exquisite piece of architecture.
Where the mountains meet the valleys makes for a picturesque view from above.
Perfect for a short hike and discovering hidden treasures. 

5. Enjoy a Luxurious Stay at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort

Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort is the best place for luxurious relaxation and allows perfect indulgence for self-pampering. Nestled in the hills, this world-class wellness retreat is known for its serene ambiance, infinity pool, spa therapies, and breathtaking views.

Why Choose Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort?

  • Aerial cable car ride to the resort – an unforgettable experience.
  • Luxurious spa treatments to relax and rejuvenate.
  • Infinity pool with a stunning mountain backdrop.
  • Gourmet dining experience with a variety of cuisines.
  • Bonfire and BBQ under the stars – perfect for an unforgettable night.

Best Time to Visit Parwanoo in Summer

  • May to July: The weather remains pleasant, with temperatures ranging between 15°C to 25°C.
  • Early mornings and evenings are cool, making it the best time for outdoor activities.

How to Reach Parwanoo?

  • By Air: The nearest airport is Chandigarh Airport (35 km away).
  • By Train: Kalka Railway Station (just 4 km away) connects Parwanoo to major cities.
  • By Road: Well-connected by road, Parwanoo is easily accessible from Delhi (270 km), Chandigarh (35 km), and Shimla (85 km).

Where to Stay in Parwanoo?

For a memorable and comfortable stay, Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort is the best choice. With its tranquil setting, luxurious amenities, and wellness treatments, it provides the ultimate relaxation experience amidst nature.

Parwanoo is the perfect summer destination for those looking for adventure, nature, and relaxation. Whether you’re planning a short getaway or a long retreat, this charming hill town offers an unforgettable experience. Book your stay at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort and indulge in the best of luxury and nature in Himachal Pradesh!  

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Why is Parwanoo famous?

Parwanoo is known for its Timber Trail cable car, scenic beauty, apple orchards, trekking trails, and luxury spa resorts like Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort.

2. What is the best time to visit Parwanoo?

The best time to visit Parwanoo is summer (May to July) and early winter (October to December) when the weather is pleasant for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

3. What are the adventure activities to do in Parwanoo?

You can enjoy cable car rides, trekking, nature walks, and exploring historical sites like Gorkha Fort.

4. How far is Parwanoo from Chandigarh?

Parwanoo is just 35 km from Chandigarh, making it a perfect weekend getaway.

5. What are the best places to stay in Parwanoo?

Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort is the best luxury stay option in Parwanoo, offering wellness treatments, an infinity pool, and a peaceful ambiance.

6. Is Parwanoo suitable for a family vacation?

Yes, Parwanoo is perfect for families as it offers scenic beauty, adventure activities, and luxurious stay options.

7. Can I visit Parwanoo for a short trip?

Yes, a 2-day trip is ideal to explore Parwanoo’s attractions like the Timber Trail, Kali Mata Temple, and Gorkha Fort.

8. How can I book a stay at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort?

You can book your stay at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort through their official website or online travel portals.
