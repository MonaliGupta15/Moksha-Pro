Mumbai to Himachal: A Rejuvenating Escape into the Mountains

By Moksha Editorial · 14 May 2024 · 824 words

Are you yearning for a break from the bustling life of Mumbai? Craving crisp mountain air, breathtaking landscapes, and soul-stirring experiences? Then, a trip to Himachal Pradesh…

Are you yearning for a break from the bustling life of Mumbai? Craving crisp mountain air, breathtaking landscapes, and soul-stirring experiences? Then, a trip to Himachal Pradesh is the perfect antidote! Nestled in the lap of the Himalayas, Himachal offers a captivating blend of natural beauty, spiritual sanctuaries, and adventure activities. This blog curates an itinerary for an unforgettable escapade from Mumbai to Himachal, with a stopover at the serene Moksha resort in Parwanoo, the midpoint of your journey.

Mumbai to Parwanoo (Solitude amidst Nature’s Embrace)

The distance between Mumbai and Parwanoo is approximately 1,800 kilometers. You can choose your travel mode based on your preference and budget. Here are the options:

  • Train: Indian Railways offer a convenient and comfortable option. Several trains connect Mumbai to Chandigarh, the nearest major railway station to Parwanoo (around 60 km away). Popular trains include the Mumbai Central-Kalka Mail and the CSMT Hazur Sahib Nanded Express.
  • Flight: Opt for a quicker journey by air. Several airlines operate flights from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (CSMIA) in Mumbai to Chandigarh Airport.
  • Road Trip: For the adventurous souls, a road trip from Mumbai to Parwanoo can be an exciting experience. The route takes you through scenic landscapes of Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, and Haryana. However, be prepared for long stretches and potential traffic congestion, especially during peak season.

Pro Tip: Regardless of your chosen mode of travel, booking your tickets well in advance, especially during peak tourist season (summer months), is recommended.

Parwanoo: A Tranquil Interlude at Moksha

After your journey from Mumbai, Parwanoo provides a perfect base to unwind and rejuvenate before embarking on your exploration of Himachal. Moksha Resort, nestled amidst the verdant hills of Parwanoo, offers a haven of tranquility. Here’s what awaits you at Moksha:

  • Serene Accommodations: Choose from well-appointed rooms, cottages, or villas, all designed to provide comfort and stunning views of the surrounding hills.
  • Rejuvenating Spa: Pamper yourself with Ayurvedic treatments or a relaxing massage at Moksha’s spa, a perfect way to de-stress after your travels.
  • Delectable Cuisine: Moksha’s in-house restaurants offer a delectable spread of Indian and international cuisines, catering to diverse palates.
  • Activities and Amenities: Take a refreshing dip in the pool, engage in recreational activities like badminton or table tennis, or simply relax amidst nature’s embrace.

Parwanoo: Unveiling Local Gems

While Parwanoo offers a tranquil escape, don’t miss exploring its local gems:

  • Parwanoo Pinjore Ropeway: Embark on a thrilling ride on the ropeway, offering panoramic views of the Shivalik Hills and the valley below.
  • Tibetan Monastery: Immerse yourself in the spiritual serenity of the Tibetan Monastery, a haven for Buddhist practices and culture.

Kasauli: Colonial Charm amidst the Hills

Kasauli, a charming hill station located around 35 kilometers from Parwanoo, is your next stop. Here’s what Kasauli has in store:

  • Colonial Architecture: Take a walk down memory lane amidst the remnants of colonial architecture, reminiscent of the British Raj era.
  • Monkey Point: Enjoy breathtaking views of the surrounding valleys and Chandigarh from Monkey Point, a popular vantage point.
  • Christ Church: Visit Christ Church, the oldest church in Kasauli, known for its neo-Gothic architecture and stained-glass windows.

Shimla: Queen of the Hills

From Kasauli, proceed to Shimla, the erstwhile summer capital of British India, located approximately 60 kilometers away. Shimla offers a captivating blend of colonial heritage, natural beauty, and vibrant culture. Here are some must-visit places:

  • The Mall Road: Take a leisurely stroll along the Mall Road, a bustling pedestrian zone lined with shops, cafes, and restaurants.
  • Viceregal Lodge: Explore the grandeur of the Viceregal Lodge, now the Indian Institute of Advanced Study, a testament to colonial architecture.
  • Himalayan Nature Park: Immerse yourself in the serene beauty of the Himalayan Nature Park, a haven for diverse flora and fauna.
  • Toy Train Ride: Embark on a nostalgic journey on the UNESCO World Heritage-listed Kalka-Shimla Railway, offering stunning mountain views.

Manali: Adventure Awaits in the Himalaya

Manali, the crown jewel of Himachal Pradesh, is located approximately 250 kilometers from Shimla. Nestled amidst snow-capped peaks, verdant valleys, and the gushing Beas River, Manali beckons adventure enthusiasts, nature lovers, and peace seekers alike. Here’s what awaits you in this captivating hill station.

  • Hidimba Devi Temple: Visit the Hidimba Devi Temple, a unique wooden temple dedicated to the wife of Bhima, one of the Pandavas from the epic Mahabharata.
  • Vashisht Temple: Immerse yourself in the spiritual aura of the Vashisht Temple, known for its natural hot water springs with purported healing properties.
  • Solang Valley: Explore the picturesque Solang Valley, a popular destination for paragliding, zorbing, and other adventure activities.
  • Old Manali: Escape the hustle and bustle of the main town and explore Old Manali, known for its laid-back cafes, traditional architecture, and stunning natural beauty.

Conclusion:

This concludes your exploration of Himachal Pradesh, from the serene stopover at Moksha in Parwanoo to the vibrant cultural tapestry and breathtaking landscapes of Kasauli, Shimla, and finally, Manali. This itinerary provides a glimpse into the diverse experiences Himachal offers.
