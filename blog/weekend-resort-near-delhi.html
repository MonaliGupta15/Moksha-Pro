Escape the Hustle: Weekend Bliss at Moksha Resort near Delhi

By Moksha Editorial · 19 May 2024 · 703 words

Delhi, the vibrant capital of India, pulsates with energy. But sometimes, the city’s constant buzz demands a serene escape. If you’re seeking a quick weekend getaway to rejuvenate your mind, body, and soul, look no further than Moksha Resort, a haven nestled amidst nature’s embrace, just a comfortable distance from Delhi.

Moksha: A Sanctuary of Tranquility

Moksha, meaning liberation in Sanskrit, perfectly embodies the resort’s philosophy. Here, you’ll find a refuge from the everyday, a place to unwind and rediscover yourself. Nestled in the foothills of the Aravalli Hills, Moksha boasts breathtaking landscapes, lush greenery, and a tranquil ambiance that washes away the city’s chaos.

Planning Your Perfect Weekend Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival and Immersion

  • Check-in (12 PM): Upon arrival at Moksha, a warm welcome awaits you. Settle into your comfortable accommodation, with options ranging from cozy cottages to luxurious suites, all designed to provide a sense of peace and comfort.

  • Afternoon Delights (1 PM – 4 PM): After a refreshing welcome drink, indulge in a delectable lunch at the resort’s in-house restaurant. Savor a variety of cuisines, from traditional Indian fare to international delicacies. Take a leisurely stroll through the resort’s landscaped gardens, allowing the fresh air and natural beauty to invigorate you.

  • Sparkling Rejuvenation (4 PM – 6 PM): Rejuvenate your body and soul at the Moksha Spa. Pamper yourself with a selection of Ayurvedic treatments or soothing massages, designed to melt away stress and restore balance.

  • Sunset Soiree (6 PM – 8 PM): As the sun dips below the horizon, painting the sky with vibrant hues, head to the resort’s poolside area. Enjoy a refreshing drink and some light snacks while soaking in the captivating sunset views. Mingle with fellow guests or simply relax with a good book, letting the serenity wash over you.

  • Culinary Canvas (8 PM onwards): Immerse yourself in a delightful dinner experience at the resort’s restaurant. Choose from a delectable à la carte menu or a buffet spread featuring regional specialties. Savor the flavors and enjoy the ambience, perfect for a romantic evening or a fun gathering with friends and family.

Day 2: Exploration and Adventure

  • Sunrise Yoga (6 AM – 7 AM): Greet the rising sun with a rejuvenating yoga session. Moksha offers guided yoga classes, suitable for all levels, allowing you to begin your day with a sense of calm and inner peace.

  • Nature’s Embrace (7 AM – 10 AM): Embark on a nature walk through the surrounding Aravalli Hills. Breathe in the fresh air, listen to the birdsong, and reconnect with nature. The resort can also arrange guided hikes or nature trails for those seeking a more adventurous exploration.

  • Culinary Delights (10 AM – 11 AM): After your invigorating morning activities, refuel with a wholesome breakfast at the resort’s restaurant. Choose from a selection of healthy options to kickstart your day.

  • Leisure Pursuits (11 AM onwards): Moksha offers a variety of activities to keep you entertained throughout the day. Take a refreshing dip in the sparkling pool, challenge yourself with a game of badminton or table tennis, or simply relax with a book by the poolside. The resort also features a children’s play area, ensuring fun for the entire family.

  • Culinary Canvas (1 PM – 2 PM): Enjoy a delicious lunch at the resort’s restaurant, offering a variety of cuisines to cater to diverse preferences.

  • Cultural Exploration (Optional – Afternoon): Venture outside the resort for an afternoon of cultural exploration. Depending on your interests, Moksha can arrange day trips to nearby historical sites, museums, or local villages, offering a glimpse into the rich heritage and vibrant culture of the region.

  • Farewell Feast (7 PM onwards): As your weekend getaway nears its end, celebrate with a final culinary treat at the resort’s restaurant. Savor a sumptuous farewell dinner, reminiscing about the memories created and the rejuvenating experience.

Beyond the Itinerary: Personalized Touches

Moksha understands that every guest is unique. To personalize your experience, the resort offers a variety of add-on activities and services. Enjoy a romantic candlelit dinner by the pool, organize a bonfire night with friends, or participate in cooking demonstrations and learn to create delectable dishes yourself. Moksha’s concierge can help curate the perfect itinerary to suit your individual needs and preferences.
