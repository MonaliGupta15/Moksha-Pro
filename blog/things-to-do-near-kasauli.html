Things to Do Near Kasauli

By Moksha Editorial · 15 Feb 2024 · 593 words

Kasauli, nestled amidst the Himalayan foothills, offers a charming escape from the hustle and bustle of city life. While the town itself boasts scenic beauty and historical treasures, its surroundings hold additional adventures waiting to be explored. So, buckle up and get ready to discover 8 incredible activities near Kasauli that will make your 2024 trip truly unforgettable:

1. Hike the Gilbert Trail: Immerse yourself in nature’s embrace on the scenic Gilbert Trail. This moderately challenging 2-km trek winds through a verdant forest, offering stunning views of the valley below. Keep your eyes peeled for diverse birdlife and colorful butterflies as you navigate the trail.

2. Seek blessings at Manki Point: Hike up to Manki Point, a popular spot known for its panoramic views and the iconic Hanuman Temple. Witness the playful monkeys residing near the temple and soak in the breathtaking vistas of the surrounding plains and hills.

3. Capture the sunset at Sunset Point: As the day draws to a close, head to Sunset Point, a picturesque location offering mesmerizing views of the valley bathed in the golden hues of the setting sun. Capture stunning photos as the sky transforms into a canvas of vibrant colors.

4. Explore the historic Christ Church: Step back in time at Christ Church, a beautiful Anglican church built in the mid-19th century. This heritage site features Gothic architecture and stained glass windows, offering a glimpse into Kasauli’s colonial past.

5. Visit the Gurkha Fort: Delve into the rich history of the region by visiting the Gurkha Fort, a 19th-century structure originally built by the Gurkha army. Explore the fort’s ramparts, cannons, and museum exhibits to gain insights into the region’s past.

6. Embark on a toy train ride: Relive your childhood memories by taking a ride on the charming toy train. This narrow-gauge railway, operational since 1907, offers a unique and scenic journey through the foothills, passing through charming villages and picturesque landscapes.

7. Shop for souvenirs at the Mall Road: Immerse yourself in the local culture by strolling down the vibrant Mall Road. Browse through the variety of shops offering souvenirs, handicrafts, and local delicacies. Indulge in some street food and soak in the bustling atmosphere of this popular tourist spot.

8.Pamper yourself at a spa resort (5 Star Resorts near Kasauli): After venturing out and exploring the region, treat yourself to a luxurious escape at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort, just a short distance from Kasauli. Nestled amidst the majestic Shivalik Ranges at an altitude of 5000 feet, this Ayurvedic Wellness Spa Resort near Kasauli (Spa Resort near Kasauli) offers the perfect blend of relaxation and rejuvenation.

Moksha Himalaya:

  • Luxurious Escape: Immerse yourself in the opulent surroundings and impeccable service at this 5-star resort near Kasauli.
  • Ayurvedic Spa: Rejuvenate your mind, body, and soul with personalized Ayurvedic spa treatments designed to promote holistic well-being.
  • Cable Car: Take a thrilling ride on the resort’s cable car, offering breathtaking aerial views of the surrounding landscape.
  • Infinity Pool: Unwind and soak in the stunning scenery while indulging in a refreshing dip in the infinity pool.
  • Culinary Delights: Tantalize your taste buds with the resort’s exquisite culinary creations, featuring both international and local delicacies.

Embrace Adventure and Relaxation:

Whether you seek adventure in the hills, spiritual solace, or a luxurious escape, the region surrounding Kasauli has something to offer everyone. So, plan your trip, choose your activities, and get ready to create lasting memories in this beautiful corner of Himachal Pradesh. Remember, after your adventures, consider pampering yourself at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort for the ultimate relaxation experience.
