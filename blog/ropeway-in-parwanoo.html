How to Reach the Parwanoo Ropeway: A Handy Guide

By Moksha Editorial · 21 Jul 2025 · 547 words

The Parwanoo ropeway or Timber Trail cable car, is located in the picturesque hills of Himachal Pradesh. It provides a stunning ride from the busy plains to the tranquil mountaintop. The cable car ride above the winding roads and pine-covered valleys is a marvel on its own. It is a must-try for all adventure and nature buffs. No matter whether you are coming from Chandigarh, Kalka or anywhere else, the journey to the ropeway is both rewarding and convenient.  

In this blog, we have covered everything from transport options to exciting rides and the best places to stay to make your mountain adventure a memorable one.   

Best Ways to Reach the Parwanoo Ropeway

A. By Road 

  • Distance from Chandigarh is 35 km. From Parwanoo, it’s a 1-hour drive. It is considered the most convenient route.

  • Chandigarh airport and Kalka railway station are 15 km away. You can hire taxi services.

  • GPS navigation leads directly to Timber Trail Resort. The base of the ropeway. 

B. By Train

  • The nearest station is Kalka (≈ 15 km). Then hire a taxi to Parwanoo.

C. By Air

  • Land at Chandigarh Airport (≈ 35–58 km away). Then use road transport to reach the ropeway base.

Step-by-Step: Reaching & Riding the Parwanoo Ropeway

  1. Start at Chandigarh/Kalka

    • From Chandigarh, hire a taxi or drive the 35 km (≈ 1 hr).

    • From Kalka Railway Station, it’s a shorter 15 km drive.

  2. Arrive at Timber Trail Resort—the ropeway base station on the Parwanoo side.

  3. Buy Ropeway Tickets

    • Pay at the base counter (~₹1,250 per adult).

    • Ticket includes the return journey and hilltop activities.

  4. Ride the Cable Car

    • Enjoy panoramic aerial views of the Shivalik range and Kaushalya River.

  5. Explore Hilltop Resort

    • Engage in zip-lining, badminton, and Burma Bridge.

    • Spend time at the restaurant/bar with valley views.

  6. Return via Ropeway

    • Board within 2 hours of ticket purchase.

    • Head back to Timber Trail Resort.

Pro Tips: Smooth Ropeway Experience

  • Start early → beat the crowd and return before 5 p.m.

  • Book accommodation at Timber Trail or Moksha for free cable car perks.

  • Parking: Available at the base resort. It is often free for guests.

  • Essentials: Bring water, light snacks, and comfortable 

  • Monsoons? Roads may be slick—choose March–June for best conditions.

Final Thoughts

  • The Parwanoo ropeway, or the Timber Trail cable car, provides an unmatched experience of a 1.8 km flight over the Shivalik.  

  • Located conveniently by road and rail from Chandigarh and Kalka.  

  • Start your ascent early. Direct bookings come with added benefits so take advantage and bring your camera!

Complete your journey with a soothing stay at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort, which is the perfect escape after a day full of exhilaration on the ropeway.
