Your Ultimate Chandigarh to Himachal Pradesh Trip Guide: Mountains, Magic, and Moksha

By Moksha Editorial · 25 Apr 2024 · 592 words

Himachal Pradesh, the “Land of Snows,” beckons with its breathtaking landscapes, charming hill stations, and rich cultural heritage. Planning a trip from Chandigarh, the vibrant capital of Punjab and Haryana, unlocks a world of adventure and serenity. This guide equips you with everything you need for an unforgettable journey, including a suggested itinerary and a delightful stopover at Moksha Spa Resort in Parwanoo.

Getting There:

Chandigarh boasts excellent connectivity. Reach the city by air (Chandigarh Airport) or train (Chandigarh Railway Station). From Chandigarh, you can embark on your Himachal adventure by:

  • Road Trip: Rent a car or hire a taxi for a scenic drive through the foothills of the Himalayas. The journey itself becomes an adventure, offering breathtaking views and charming stopovers. Popular routes include Chandigarh-Shimla and Chandigarh-Manali.
  • Bus: Himachal Road Transport Corporation (HRTC) offers reliable and affordable bus services connecting Chandigarh with various destinations in Himachal Pradesh.

Tailoring Your Trip:

Himachal Pradesh caters to diverse interests. Here’s how to tailor your itinerary:

  • Nature Lovers: Trek through scenic valleys, explore serene lakes, and witness snow-capped peaks. Popular destinations include Manali, McLeodGanj, and Spiti Valley.
  • Adventure Seekers: Go paragliding in Bir Billing, white-water rafting on the Beas River, or mountain biking in Shimla.
  • Spiritual Seekers: Visit ancient temples like Hidimba Devi Temple in Manali and monasteries like Tsuglagkhang Complex in Dharamshala.
  • History Buffs: Explore historical sites like Kangra Fort and the Viceregal Lodge in Shimla.

A Sample Itinerary (7 Days):

Day 1: Start your journey from Chandigarh. Consider a stopover in Parwanoo (details below).

Day 2: Reach Shimla, the “Queen of Hills.” Explore colonial architecture, stroll on The Mall, and take in panoramic views from Chadwick Falls.

Day 3: Embark on a day trip to Kufri, a charming hill station offering breathtaking Himalayan views, horseback riding, and yak rides.

Day 4: Travel to Manali, a popular hill station known for its adventure activities, apple orchards, and Tibetan influence. Visit the Hidimba Devi Temple and Vashisht hot springs.

Day 5: Explore Solang Valley, a haven for paragliding, skiing (during winter), and mountain biking.

Day 6: Travel to Dharamshala, the “Little Lhasa” of India. Visit the Dalai Lama’s residence, Tsuglagkhang Complex, explore the vibrant McLeodGanj market, and soak in the panoramic views.

Day 7: Return to Chandigarh, stopping at souvenir shops along the way.

A Perfect Parwanoo Stopover (Day 1):

Nestled amidst the Shivalik Hills, Parwanoo offers a delightful stopover on your way to Himachal. Consider staying at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort, a haven of tranquility and rejuvenation.

Why Moksha Spa Resort?

  • Location: Just a short drive from Chandigarh, Moksha provides the perfect break from the city bustle before venturing deeper into Himachal.
  • Scenic Beauty: Surrounded by lush greenery and captivating mountain views, Moksha offers a taste of the tranquility that awaits you in Himachal.
  • Luxurious Accommodation: Choose from spacious rooms or opulent suites, all designed for comfort and relaxation.
  • Rejuvenating Spa: Pamper yourself with a variety of Ayurvedic and international spa treatments before starting your adventure-filled journey.
  • Culinary Delights: Moksha’s restaurants offer a delectable spread of Indian and international cuisines, catering to all palates.
  • Activities: Enjoy a refreshing dip in the pool, unwind in the game zone, or explore the surrounding hills with a nature walk.

Activities in Parwanoo:

While enjoying the tranquility of Moksha, don’t miss exploring Parwanoo’s hidden gems:

  • Parwanoo Pinjore Garden: A terraced garden showcasing Mughal influence, perfect for a stroll amidst scenic beauty.
  • Tibetan Handicrafts Market: Pick up unique souvenirs and handicrafts from the vibrant Tibetan market.
  • Rock Garden: Explore the quirky sculptures and artistic creations at the Cactus Garden.
