A Long Weekend near Chandigarh: Rejuvenation at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort near Chandigarh

Craving a break from the city grind but short on time? Look no further than a revitalizing long weekend near Chandigarh. Nestled amidst the verdant Shivalik Hills in Parwanoo, the Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort offers an idyllic escape, promising serenity, rejuvenation, and a taste of Himalayan luxury.

Why Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort for Your Long Weekend?

When it comes to the best long weekend near Chandigarh, Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort ticks all the boxes. Here’s why:

  • Tranquil Getaway: A mere 35 minutes from Chandigarh International Airport, Moksha Himalaya whisks you away from the urban chaos. Nestled at an elevation of 5200 feet, the resort boasts breathtaking Himalayan vistas, creating an atmosphere of serenity perfect for unwinding.

  • Ayurvedic Rejuvenation: Indulge in the ancient practice of Ayurveda at Moksha’s renowned spa. Rejuvenate your mind, body, and spirit with personalized treatments, massages, and therapies designed to restore balance and well-being.

  • Luxurious Accommodations: Spoil yourself with Moksha’s exquisitely designed suites. Each suite offers breathtaking views, plush amenities, and a private balcony, allowing you to truly connect with the surrounding nature.

  • Activities for All: Whether you seek adventure or relaxation, Moksha caters to all. Take a dip in the infinity pool overlooking the valley, explore the surrounding hills on a nature walk, or challenge yourself with a trek. The resort also offers yoga sessions, meditation classes, and cultural evenings.

  • Culinary Delights: Embark on a culinary journey at Moksha’s restaurants. Savor delectable Indian and international cuisines prepared with fresh, local ingredients.

Planning Your Long Weekend Escape:

Long Weekends: Take advantage of Indian holidays like Independence Day (August 15th) or Gandhi Jayanti (October 2nd) that fall on a Tuesday or Wednesday to create a long weekend. Alternatively, weekends with public holidays on a Friday or Monday offer another perfect opportunity.

Places to Visit Near Chandigarh: While Moksha Himalaya provides a complete escape, if you’re looking to explore the surrounding area, here are some options:

  • Kasauli: This charming hill station, just 30 km from Moksha, offers stunning views, colonial architecture, and a peaceful ambiance.

  • Shimla: The “Queen of Hills,” Shimla is a popular tourist destination known for its colonial buildings, the Mall Road, and scenic beauty (approximately 70 km from Moksha).

  • Pinjore Gardens: Explore these Mughal-era gardens, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, boasting scenic beauty and historical significance (approximately 20 km from Moksha).

Making the Most of Your Long Weekend:

Here’s a suggested itinerary to maximize your long weekend at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort:

  • Day 1: Arrive at the resort, check into your luxurious suite, and take in the breathtaking views. Unwind by the infinity pool or indulge in a rejuvenating spa treatment. In the evening, enjoy a delectable meal at one of the resort’s restaurants.

  • Day 2: Start your day with a yoga session or a nature walk. Explore the surrounding hills or visit Kasauli for a day trip. In the afternoon, pamper yourself with another spa treatment or try your hand at an activity like ziplining or mountain biking (offered by some local operators).

  • Day 3: Indulge in a leisurely breakfast and soak in the mountain views from your balcony. Take a dip in the pool or enjoy some retail therapy at the resort’s boutique. In the evening, savor a farewell dinner and cherish the memories made.

A Long Weekend to Remember:

Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort offers the perfect escape for a long weekend near Chandigarh. With its focus on wellness, luxurious accommodations, and stunning surroundings, it allows you to truly disconnect, rejuvenate, and return to the city feeling refreshed and revitalized. So, pack your bags, escape the ordinary, and embark on a journey of rejuvenation at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort.
