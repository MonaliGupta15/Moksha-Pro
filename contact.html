Getting here

The last nine
minutes are by air.

The resort sits at 5,000 ft, reached by cable car from the Timber Trail base. Tell us when you are coming and we will time the ascent for you.

01

Chandigarh airport

Approximately 45 minutes by road to the Timber Trail base at Parwanoo.

02

Kalka railway station

Approximately 20 minutes by road. Trains from Delhi and Shimla connect here.

03

Timber Trail base

Park your car, check in at the desk, and board the cable car between 9 am and 6 pm.

04

Moksha

Nine minutes above the valley. A welcome drink, your suite, and the horizon.
