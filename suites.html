The idea

Space, light, silence

Nestled in the peaceful embrace of the Shivalik forests, our suites are designed to let you drift into deep, comfortable sleep — soothed by the silence of the Himalayas.

“Every window is a decision: to let the mountain in, and to leave the rest of the world outside.”

Whether you are drawn to the twinkling lights of the distant city or the untouched beauty of Himachal's ridgelines, the rooms are placed to give you an unobstructed view. Every morning begins with a scene from nature's own canvas.
