The rhythm

Make time disappear

A cable car through the sky, an infinity pool at the edge of the world, live music under a star-filled canopy. At Moksha, the days are full of nothing in particular — and that is the point.

“You don’t come here to fill your schedule. You come here to empty it.”

Whether you are hiking pine-covered ridgelines, playing tennis amidst the fresh alpine breeze, or floating in heated waters as the sun slips behind the Shivalik peaks, every moment is designed to quiet the mind.

Ever imagined soaring through the sky? At Moksha, check-in starts with a cable car ride from the Timber Trail base — nine minutes of panoramic aerial views that are nothing short of awe-inspiring.
