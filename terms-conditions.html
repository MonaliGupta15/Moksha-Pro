Resort Protocols

Clarity, comfort & care

Because Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort is perched at 5,000 feet and reached via a nine-minute aerial cable-car journey over the valley, careful coordination ensures your safety and serenity.

“A peaceful stay begins with clear expectations. Every guideline exists to protect the quiet sanctuary of the mountain.”

By confirming a room reservation, dining booking, or event contract with Asia Resorts Limited, guests agree to abide by the following operational policies and house regulations.

01

Cable Car Transfer & Arrival Timings

Operating Hours: The aerial cable car operates strictly between 09:30 hrs and 17:30 hrs daily. Guests booked for Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort are requested to plan their arrival at the Timber Trail base station and their final departure within these prescribed hours only.

Late Arrival / Weather Advisory: In the event of an arrival after 17:30 hrs, the resort cannot operate the cable car after dark and provides no alternative transit or off-site accommodation; such reservations will be treated as a no-show. Furthermore, cable car operations remain subject to prevailing mountain weather conditions and technical safety standards.

02

Reservation Guarantee & Advance Payment

Quoted rates are indicative offers and do not hold inventory without formal confirmation. Room reservations are confirmed solely against 100% advance payment in favor of Asia Resorts Limited prior to the designated deadline.

In the event of non-payment by the due date, reserved rooms will be automatically released without prior notice, and fresh bookings will be subject strictly to prevailing tariff and availability.

03

Check-In, Check-Out & Identification

Timings: Check-in time is 14:00 hrs; Check-out time is 12:00 hrs (noon). Early check-in or late check-out is subject to room availability on the date and attracts supplementary charges.

Government ID Requirement: In compliance with Government of India and local authorities, all adult guests (Indian and foreign nationals) must carry and present a valid government-issued photo identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License) upon arrival at the reception desk.

04

Taxes, Additional Guests & Wi-Fi

Applicable Taxes: Prevailing statutory GST (currently 18%) is applicable on room tariff, food, and beverage services, and will be charged extra as per government notifications.

Additional Occupants: Additional children, extra adult guests, rollaway beds, and meals not specified at the time of initial booking will be billed separately at check-in.

Wi-Fi Access: High-speed internet is available across the resort at an additional charge of INR 590/- per device / day.

05

House Rules, Swimming Pool & Outside Food

Outside Food & Alcohol: To maintain food hygiene and licensing protocols, outside eatables, delivery foods, and personal liquor are strictly prohibited on hotel premises.

Infinity Pool & Jacuzzis: Use of the temperature-controlled infinity pool and outdoor hot jacuzzis is permitted strictly in proper swimwear attire. Glassware is prohibited in wet pool zones.

Spa Appointments: Because treatment slots in our Ayurvedic center and Turkish Hammam are limited, pre-booking appointments at least 24 hours in advance is strongly recommended.

06

Cancellation, Amendments & No-Show Policy

14+ Days Notice: Cancellations or amendments received 14 or more days prior to the scheduled arrival date receive a refund, subject to administrative charges.

Within 14 Days: Cancellations or amendments received within 14 days of arrival attract 100% retention charge for the entire booked stay.

No-Show or Early Departure: No refunds, credits, or adjustments will be issued for early checkout or failure to arrive due to transit delays or weather.
