The Signature Wellness Escape

Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort

Suspended in the quiet clouds of Parwanoo, Moksha is our flagship Ayurvedic wellness retreat. Accessible exclusively via a 9-minute cable-car ascent over the valley, it offers all-suite luxury, North India's largest Hammam, and a heated infinity pool touching the mountain sky.

The Panoramic Mountain Retreat

Timber Trail Heights & Terraces

Perched along the high crest of the Shivaliks adjacent to the cable-car terminal, Heights & Terraces commands a sweeping 360-degree panorama of Himachal. Renowned for terraced valley balconies, destination weddings in the Mansion Hall, and state-of-the-art conference conclaves.

The River Valley Gateway

Timber Trail Resort

Nestled at the serene foothills along the Kaushalya River, Timber Trail Resort is the welcoming highway gateway to Parwanoo. Sprawling pine-shaded lawns, riverside dining, children's recreation, and the historic launchpad terminal for the aerial cable car up to the heights.

Abhyanga

A synchronised, full-body massage with warm herbal oil, chosen for your dosha and poured slowly enough that the mind has no choice but to follow the body into rest.

60 / 90 minutesAyurveda

Shirodhara

A continuous thread of warm oil is poured across the forehead. Thought slows. The nervous system remembers what quiet feels like. One of Ayurveda's most profound rituals.

45 minutesSignature
03

The Hammam

The largest Hammam in North India. Heat, steam, black soap and a vigorous kessa glove — an ancient cleansing ritual reimagined in marble and candlelight.

90 minutesHammam

Infinity Pool

A blade of warm water that ends where the mountains begin. Swim at dawn, float at noon, and watch the sky turn copper from the edge of the world.

Guests onlyOpen 7 am – 7 pm
