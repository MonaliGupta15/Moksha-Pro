Privacy & Data

How we protect your trust

This Privacy Policy explains how Asia Resorts Limited collects, safeguards, and utilizes your personal information across your digital interactions with mokshaspa.com and your physical stay at Moksha Himalaya Spa Resort.

“Discretion is the quietest form of luxury. Your personal details are never sold, traded, or compromised.”

By accessing our website or reserving accommodation, spa services, or event spaces, you acknowledge and agree to the policies and privacy safeguards described below.

01

Information We Collect

Personal Information: When you make a reservation, submit an enquiry, book a spa therapy, or contact our reservations desk, we collect identifiable information such as your full name, email address, telephone contact number, residential address, and specific stay preferences.

Banking & Payment Safeguards: We do not store your complete banking credentials or credit/debit card numbers directly on our servers. All digital monetary transactions are processed through authorized, RBI-compliant payment gateway partners utilizing industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption and PCI-DSS compliance.

Technical & Non-Personal Information: We may collect non-personally identifiable technical information including browser type, operating system, IP address, and anonymized browsing behavior to optimize site responsiveness and user experience.

02

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly utilized for legitimate hospitality purposes:

  • Processing room reservations, packages, spa treatments, and cable-car transit passes.
  • Communicating arrival itineraries, weather updates, and check-in requirements.
  • Tailoring personalized dietary, wellness, and suite preferences during your stay.
  • Complying with statutory guest reporting regulations established by the Government of India and the Himachal Pradesh Tourism Department.
03

Cookie Policy & Analytics

Our website uses first-party and performance cookies to enhance navigation, retain preference states (such as selected check-in dates), and monitor aggregate traffic. You can modify your browser settings at any time to reject cookies; however, certain interactive booking engine features may require cookies to function correctly.

04

Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal details to outside commercial third parties. Your data is only shared with authorized service partners (such as reservation engine operators and secure payment processors) strictly to fulfill your booking, or when formally required by law enforcement or governmental authorities.

05

Your Rights & Data Control

You have the absolute right to inspect, correct, or request deletion of the personal information stored in our reservations registry. To request a copy of your records or to request removal from promotional communications, please email us directly at reservations@mokshaspa.com.

06

Contact for Privacy Inquiries
