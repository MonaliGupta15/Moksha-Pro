Weddings · reunions · MICE

Your love story is unique, and your wedding should be too. Our Mansion Hall and Royal Ballroom offer flexible, customisable celebrations — traditional or contemporary, intimate or expansive.

“Let us do the rest. Every detail, from the first welcome to the last dance, is handled with the same quiet care.”

Well-equipped with a fully electronic dance floor and modern facilities, the Mansion Hall accommodates around 350 guests; the Royal Ballroom up to 150. For corporate retreats, the 2,095 sq ft Conference Hall at Timber Trail Heights blends sophistication, comfort and function.
